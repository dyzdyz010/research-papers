# Academic Papers on European Geopolitics
# 欧洲地缘政治学术论文

## Overview 概述

This document compiles key academic papers on European geopolitics, covering strategic autonomy, EU foreign and security policy, transatlantic relations, EU-Russia relations, EU-China relations, European defence integration, EU enlargement, and European energy security.

本文档汇编了欧洲地缘政治领域的重要学术论文，涵盖战略自主、欧盟外交与安全政策、跨大西洋关系、欧俄关系、欧中关系、欧洲防务一体化、欧盟扩大及欧洲能源安全等主题。

## 1. European Strategic Autonomy (欧洲战略自主)

#### Paper 1: European Strategic Autonomy
- **Authors**: Amelie Theussen
- **Journal**: European Strategic Autonomy and Small States' Security (Routledge)
- **Year**: 2022
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.4324/9781003324867-10
- **Abstract**: This chapter examines the concept of European strategic autonomy from the perspective of small states' security concerns, analyzing how the pursuit of strategic autonomy affects the security architecture of European small states.

#### Paper 2: European Strategic Autonomy: Conceptualizing the Framework
- **Authors**: Giedrius Česnakas
- **Journal**: European Strategic Autonomy and Small States' Security (Routledge)
- **Year**: 2022
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.4324/9781003324867-2
- **Abstract**: Provides a comprehensive conceptual framework for understanding European strategic autonomy, exploring its theoretical foundations and practical implications for European integration.

#### Paper 3: Strategic Autonomy Through External Differentiation
- **Authors**: Pernille Rieker, Mathilde T. E. Giske
- **Journal**: European Actorness in a Shifting Geopolitical Order (Springer)
- **Year**: 2023
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-44546-0_5
- **Abstract**: Explores how the EU engages with non-member states through differentiated integration mechanisms and how this contributes to European strategic autonomy. Examines bilateral and multilateral agreements between the EU and associated non-members.

#### Paper 4: European Strategic Autonomy in Lithuania's Foreign Policy Discourse
- **Authors**: Ieva Karpavičiūtė
- **Journal**: European Strategic Autonomy and Small States' Security (Routledge)
- **Year**: 2022
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.4324/9781003324867-9
- **Abstract**: Analyzes how European strategic autonomy is perceived and discussed within Lithuanian foreign policy discourse, highlighting the tension between Atlanticism and European integration.

#### Paper 5: The Emergence of 'Strategic Autonomy' in EU-NATO Relations
- **Authors**: Jolyon Howorth
- **Journal**: Security and Defence Policy in the European Union (Bloomsbury)
- **Year**: 2014
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-137-42788-5_4
- **Abstract**: Examines the evolution of strategic autonomy concept within the context of EU-NATO relations and transatlantic security cooperation.

#### Paper 6: Digital Sovereignty and European Strategic Autonomy
- **Authors**: Julia Pohle, Thorsten Thiel
- **Journal**: Internet Policy Review
- **Year**: 2020
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.14763/2020.4.1532
- **Abstract**: Over the last decade, digital sovereignty has become a central element in policy discourses on digital issues. The paper systematizes the various normative claims to digital sovereignty and shows how the concept is understood as a discursive practice in politics and policy.

## 2. EU Common Foreign and Security Policy (欧盟共同外交与安全政策)

#### Paper 7: The Common Foreign and Security Policy (CFSP): A Component of EU Foreign Action
- **Authors**: Various
- **Journal**: European Union Law (Routledge)
- **Year**: 2013
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.4324/9780203597866-19
- **Abstract**: Comprehensive analysis of CFSP as a component of EU's external action, examining its legal basis, institutional framework, and practical implementation.

#### Paper 8: The Common Foreign and Security Policy (CFSP)
- **Authors**: Stephan Keukeleire, Tom Delreux
- **Journal**: The Foreign Policy of the European Union (Macmillan)
- **Year**: 2014
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-137-02576-0_8
- **Abstract**: Provides a systematic overview of CFSP, analyzing its evolution, instruments, decision-making processes, and key policy areas.

#### Paper 9: Representing the EU in the Area of CFSP: Legal and Political Dynamics
- **Authors**: Hylke Dijkstra, Peter Van Elsuwege
- **Journal**: Research Handbook on the EU's Common Foreign and Security Policy (Edward Elgar)
- **Year**: 2018
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.4337/9781785364082.00010
- **Abstract**: Examines the legal and political dynamics of EU representation in the CFSP area, analyzing the roles of different EU institutions and actors.

#### Paper 10: European Neighbourhood Policy: CFSP in Disguise
- **Authors**: Steven Blockmans
- **Journal**: Research Handbook on the EU's Common Foreign and Security Policy (Edward Elgar)
- **Year**: 2018
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.4337/9781785364082.00024
- **Abstract**: Analyzes the relationship between the European Neighbourhood Policy and CFSP, arguing that ENP serves as a vehicle for extending EU foreign policy objectives.

#### Paper 11: EU Development Cooperation and the CFSP: Mutual Encroachment?
- **Authors**: Morten Broberg
- **Journal**: Research Handbook on the EU's Common Foreign and Security Policy (Edward Elgar)
- **Year**: 2018
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.4337/9781785364082.00021
- **Abstract**: Explores the intersection between EU development cooperation and CFSP, examining areas of overlap and potential tension.

#### Paper 12: Foreign Policy Action in CFSP
- **Authors**: Dimitrios Kavakas
- **Journal**: Greece and Spain in European Foreign Policy (Routledge)
- **Year**: 2020
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.4324/9781003073406-7
- **Abstract**: Case study analysis of how CFSP is implemented through the foreign policy actions of member states, using Greece and Spain as examples.

## 3. Transatlantic Relations (跨大西洋关系)

#### Paper 13: 'Rogue States' and Transatlantic Relations
- **Authors**: Philip H. Gordon
- **Journal**: The United States and Europe in the Global Arena (Palgrave Macmillan)
- **Year**: 1999
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1057/9780230379763_5
- **Abstract**: Analyzes transatlantic disagreements over policy toward so-called "rogue states," examining how different approaches to Iran, Iraq, and other states have created tensions in US-European relations.

#### Paper 14: Transatlantic Relations in a Global Economy
- **Authors**: Various
- **Journal**: The United States and Europe (Routledge)
- **Year**: 2007
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.4324/9780203027578-18
- **Abstract**: Examines the economic dimensions of transatlantic relations, analyzing trade, investment, and regulatory cooperation between the US and Europe.

#### Paper 15: Techno-Transatlantic: Science and Technology in Relations Between the United States and Europe
- **Authors**: Małgorzata Zachara
- **Journal**: Ad Americam
- **Year**: 2018
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.12797/adamericam.18.2017.18.07
- **Abstract**: Focuses on technology as a factor in twentieth-century relations between the United States and Europe, demonstrating how technology development provides structure for transatlantic cooperation.

#### Paper 16: Norway and Transatlantic Relations: From Predictability to Uncertainty
- **Authors**: Pernille Rieker
- **Journal**: The United States and the Future of Europe (Springer)
- **Year**: 2025
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-83350-2_36
- **Abstract**: Examines Norway's role in transatlantic relations and how recent geopolitical changes have affected the traditionally stable Norwegian-American security partnership.

#### Paper 17: Selling it to Uncle Sam… CSDP and Transatlantic Relations
- **Authors**: Jolyon Howorth
- **Journal**: Security and Defence Policy in the European Union (Bloomsbury)
- **Year**: 2014
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-137-42788-5_4
- **Abstract**: Analyzes how the development of EU's Common Security and Defence Policy has affected transatlantic relations, particularly with the United States.

#### Paper 18: The Old and the New: Germany, Poland and the Recalibration of Transatlantic Security Relations
- **Authors**: Various
- **Journal**: The United States and Europe (Routledge)
- **Year**: 2007
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.4324/9780203027578-10
- **Abstract**: Examines how Germany and Poland have navigated transatlantic security relations, particularly in the context of EU enlargement and evolving NATO structures.

## 4. EU-Russia Relations (欧俄关系)

#### Paper 19: EU-Russia Energy Relations in Context
- **Authors**: Roland Dannreuther
- **Journal**: Geopolitics
- **Year**: 2016
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1080/14650045.2016.1222521
- **Abstract**: Provides contextual analysis of EU-Russia energy relations, examining the geopolitical factors that shape energy cooperation and conflict.

#### Paper 20: The Shifting Geopolitics of Russia's Natural Gas Exports and Their Impact on EU-Russia Gas Relations
- **Authors**: Jack D. Sharples
- **Journal**: Geopolitics
- **Year**: 2016
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1080/14650045.2016.1148690
- **Abstract**: Analyzes the changing geopolitics of Russian natural gas exports and their implications for EU-Russia gas relations, considering factors such as pipeline politics and market dynamics.

#### Paper 21: Great Game or Great Confusion: The Geopolitical Understanding of EU-Russia Energy Relations
- **Authors**: Tom Casier
- **Journal**: Geopolitics
- **Year**: 2016
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1080/14650045.2016.1185607
- **Abstract**: Critically examines the geopolitical framing of EU-Russia energy relations, questioning whether the "Great Game" metaphor accurately captures the complexity of these relationships.

#### Paper 22: Challenging Reductionism in Analyses of EU-Russia Energy Relations
- **Authors**: Andrew Judge, Tomas Maltby, Jack D. Sharples
- **Journal**: Geopolitics
- **Year**: 2016
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1080/14650045.2016.1222520
- **Abstract**: Argues against reductionist approaches to analyzing EU-Russia energy relations, advocating for more nuanced understanding of the multifaceted nature of these relationships.

#### Paper 23: The Future of EU-Russia Energy Relations in the Context of Decarbonisation
- **Authors**: Olga Khrushcheva, Tomas Maltby
- **Journal**: Geopolitics
- **Year**: 2016
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1080/14650045.2016.1188081
- **Abstract**: Explores how decarbonisation policies may transform EU-Russia energy relations in the future, considering the implications for both parties.

#### Paper 24: EU Member States' Energy Relations with Russia: Conflicting Approaches to Securing Natural Gas Supplies
- **Authors**: Anke Schmidt-Felzmann
- **Journal**: Geopolitics
- **Year**: 2011
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1080/14650045.2011.520864
- **Abstract**: Examines the divergent approaches of EU member states toward energy relations with Russia, analyzing how national interests affect the development of a common EU energy policy.

#### Paper 25: Hybrid Geopolitics in EU-Russia Relations: Understanding the Persistence of Conflict and Cooperation
- **Authors**: Cristian Nitoiu, Florin Pasatoiu
- **Journal**: Cooperation and Conflict between Europe and Russia (Routledge)
- **Year**: 2021
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.4324/9781003202349-3
- **Abstract**: Introduces the concept of "hybrid geopolitics" to explain the coexistence of conflict and cooperation in EU-Russia relations.

## 6. European Defence Integration (欧洲防务一体化)

#### Paper 31: The Common Security and Defence Policy (CSDP) – Challenges after "Lisbon"
- **Authors**: Gunther Hauser
- **Journal**: The European Union – A Global Actor? (Verlag Barbara Budrich)
- **Year**: 2012
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.2307/j.ctvdf0fhc.5
- **Abstract**: Examines the challenges facing CSDP after the Lisbon Treaty, analyzing institutional changes and their implications for European defence cooperation.

#### Paper 32: Introduction: CSDP — A 'Work in Progress'
- **Authors**: Jolyon Howorth
- **Journal**: Security and Defence Policy in the European Union (Bloomsbury)
- **Year**: 2014
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-137-42788-5_1
- **Abstract**: Provides an overview of CSDP as an evolving policy area, examining its development, achievements, and remaining challenges.

#### Paper 33: The Common Security and Defence Policy (CSDP)
- **Authors**: Stephan Keukeleire, Tom Delreux
- **Journal**: The Foreign Policy of the European Union (Macmillan)
- **Year**: 2014
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-137-02576-0_9
- **Abstract**: Comprehensive analysis of CSDP, examining its institutional framework, capabilities, missions, and relationship with NATO.

#### Paper 34: The Times Are Changing: Resetting CSDP and European Defence
- **Authors**: Francisco De Borja Lasheras
- **Journal**: Cuadernos Europeos de Deusto
- **Year**: 2014
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.18543/ced-50-2014pp77-103
- **Abstract**: Argues that CSDP faces a strategic moment requiring recalibration, analyzing the implications of US rebalancing toward Asia and European fiscal constraints.

#### Paper 35: Analyzing the European Union's CSDP and the Evolution of European Security and Defense Training at the ESDC
- **Authors**: João Reis
- **Journal**: Defence Studies
- **Year**: 2025
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1080/14702436.2024.2417959
- **Abstract**: Examines the evolution of CSDP and the role of the European Security and Defence College in developing European security and defence capabilities.

#### Paper 36: Quo Vadis, CSDP?
- **Authors**: Felix Biermann
- **Journal**: The European Union in International Affairs (Springer)
- **Year**: 2023
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-30054-7_10
- **Abstract**: Explores the future direction of CSDP in an increasingly complex international regime environment.
