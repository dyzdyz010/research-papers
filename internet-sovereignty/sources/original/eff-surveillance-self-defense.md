# Surveillance Self-Defense

Source: https://ssd.eff.org/

---

We're the Electronic Frontier Foundation, a [member-supported](https://supporters.eff.org/donate/support-surveillance-self-defense) non-profit working to protect online privacy for over thirty-five years. This is Surveillance Self-Defense: our expert guide to protecting you and your friends from online spying.

Read the [BASICS](/module-categories/basics) to find out how online surveillance works. Dive into our [TOOL GUIDES](/module-categories/tool-guides) for instructions to installing our pick of the best, most secure applications. We have more detailed information in our [FURTHER LEARNING](/module-categories/further-learning) sections. If you'd like a guided tour, look for our list of common [SECURITY SCENARIOS](/module-categories/security-scenarios).

## Main Sections

- **BASICS** - Learn how online surveillance works
- **TOOL GUIDES** - Instructions for installing secure applications
- **FURTHER LEARNING** - More detailed information on digital security
- **SECURITY SCENARIOS** - Guided tours for common security situations
