# Academic Journals Information

## 1. Journal of Cybersecurity (Oxford University Press)

**Website:** https://academic.oup.com/cybersecurity

### Overview
The Journal of Cybersecurity is an open access peer-reviewed academic journal of cybersecurity. It is published by Oxford University Press. It was first issued in 2015.

### Editorial Leadership
- **Editors-in-Chief:** Tyler Moore and David Pym

### Scope and Focus
The journal concentrates on the belief that computer science approaches are critical, but are not enough to tackle cybersecurity threats. Moreover, the journal maintains the belief that interdisciplinary academic contributions are needed to understand the different facets of cybersecurity.

### Publication Details
- **Language:** English
- **Access Model:** Open Access
- **Publisher:** Oxford University Press (United Kingdom)
- **ISSN (Print):** 2057-2085
- **ISSN (Online):** 2057-2093
- **ISO 4 Abbreviation:** J. Cybersecur.
- **First Published:** 2015

### Ethics and Standards
The journal is a member of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE).

## 2. Internet Policy Review

**Website:** https://policyreview.info/

### Overview
Internet Policy Review is a diamond open access and peer-reviewed online journal on internet regulation in Europe.

### Scope
Internet Policy Review tracks public regulatory changes as well as private policy developments which are expected to have long lasting impacts on European societies. The journal is a resource on internet policy for academics, civil society advocates, entrepreneurs, the media and policymakers alike. Internet Policy Review contributes empirical research, analysis and current affairs coverage to contemporary debate about media, information technology, telecommunications and internet governance. Internet Policy Review's expertise resides in its clear and independent analysis of inter-European and pan-European digital policy changes.

### Impact and Ranking
- **Scimago Journal Rank (2024):** 1.263
- **Quartile Ranking:** Since 2022, continuously ranked in the first quartile (Q1) in the fields of:
  1. Communication
  2. Computer Networks and Communication
  3. Management, Monitoring, Policy and Law

### Peer Review Process
The peer review process is not blind but transparent. Authors, reviewers, and editors work collaboratively on the same copy of the manuscript. The editorial team handpicks two reviewers for each manuscript:
- One reviewer is directly involved in the academic field matching the specific issue
- The second reviewer generally comes from a different country, specialised in a complementary academic field

### Key Features
- **Quality peer review:** Transparent and constructive process
- **Trans-disciplinarity:** Collaborators interact with peers from various fields including social sciences, computer science, economics, law, communication studies, cultural studies, history, information engineering, linguistics, and psychology
- **Plural audience:** Serves academics and non-academics alike
- **Contextual publishing:** Covers current normative and regulatory developments

### Funding
Internet Policy Review is a publication of the Alexander von Humboldt Institute for Internet and Society. The journal has publishing cooperation with:

## 3. Telecommunications Policy

**Website:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/telecommunications-policy

### Overview
**The International Journal of Digital Economy, Data Sciences and New Media**

Telecommunications Policy is concerned with the impact of digitalization in the economy and society. The journal is multidisciplinary, encompassing conceptual, theoretical and empirical studies, quantitative as well as qualitative.

### Scope
The scope includes:
- Policy, regulation, and governance
- Big data, artificial intelligence and data science
- New and traditional sectors encompassing new media and the platform economy
- Management, entrepreneurship, innovation and use

Contributions may explore these topics at national, regional and international levels, including issues confronting both developed and developing countries. The papers accepted by the journal meet high standards of analytical rigor and policy relevance.

### Publication Details
- **Publisher:** Elsevier
- **Online ISSN:** 1879-3258
- **Print ISSN:** 0308-5961
- **Editor-in-Chief:** Erik Bohlin, PhD (Chalmers University of Technology, Gothenburg, Sweden)

### Publishing Options
